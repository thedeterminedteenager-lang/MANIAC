Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MANIAC handles information when visitors use our website, including MANIAC AI and our speed-test tool. Please read it together with any notices shown within those services.

1. Information processed when you visit

MANIAC is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting infrastructure may also process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. For more information, read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. MANIAC AI conversations

When you use MANIAC AI, the messages you submit and the AI responses may be stored by MANIAC's application or its underlying service. Conversation data may be processed to generate responses and maintain the feature. The actual retention period, access controls and any sharing depend on the service and its configuration.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, private identification information or other sensitive information to MANIAC AI. We will update this section with the applicable service provider's data practices and retention details once verified.

3. Speed-test tool and external services

MANIAC's speed-test tool may request data from external services to measure network performance and identify the internet provider or approximate location associated with a connection. These requests can expose technical information, such as your IP address, to the relevant service.

Where the tool uses ipwho.is for network lookup, that provider receives the information required to handle the request. Review ipwho.is and its applicable privacy information for details about its handling of requests. The speed-test service may also receive connection information needed to run a test.

4. Analytics and cookies

MANIAC does not intentionally use a separate website analytics service at the time of this policy's publication. This does not mean that the hosting provider or external services never process technical information.

If advertising, embedded content or other third-party features are added, those services may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies according to their own practices and settings.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense is enabled on MANIAC, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and, depending on applicable settings and user choices, personalise ads.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also visit YourAdChoices for information about choices offered by participating companies.

Advertising is not currently represented as active merely by the presence of this policy. If advertising is introduced, this policy and any required consent notices will be reviewed and updated.

6. Third-party services

Some MANIAC features rely on services operated by other organisations. Those organisations may process information when your browser communicates with them. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

7. Children's privacy

MANIAC is not intended to encourage children to share sensitive personal information. Please avoid submitting private information through the AI feature or other interactive tools.

8. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use MANIAC AI or external lookup features. You can also use your browser settings to manage cookies. Some browser or service settings may affect how features work.

For questions about this policy or requests concerning information handled by MANIAC, use the contact method published on the MANIAC website. Requests involving an external provider may need to be directed to that provider.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MANIAC's features, service providers, advertising arrangements or legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
