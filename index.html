ABOUT MUHAMMAD

MUHAMMAD is a young technology enthusiast, creator, and aspiring entrepreneur with a passion for understanding how technology works and imagining what it could become.

From smartphones and computers to gaming, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, web development, and digital creativity, MUHAMMAD is constantly exploring new ideas and experimenting with technology. His interest goes beyond simply using technology — he wants to understand it, explain it, improve it, and eventually build it.

That vision led to MANIAC, a technology brand concept created to bring together innovative products and ideas across smartphones, laptops, tablets, gaming, AI, displays, networking, and more.

Alongside his technology ambitions, MUHAMMAD is also interested in content creation and video editing, creating technology-focused content and sharing interesting ideas with others. He believes that great technology should not only be powerful, but also exciting, accessible, and designed with the future in mind.

His long-term ambition is to grow MANIAC into a major global technology company — building products, developing new ideas, and creating an ecosystem that can stand alongside the world's biggest technology brands.
